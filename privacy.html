Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

The Short Version

Zoner does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The developer never receives any of your data.

What Stays on Your Device

  • Your Tradier API token is stored in your device’s Keychain, on that device only (not synced).
  • Your list of recent symbols and your settings are stored on your device only.
  • Price data is kept in memory and is gone when the app quits.

Deleting the app removes all of it.

Who the App Talks To

To show market data, the app sends requests directly from your device, with no server in between, to Tradier, for price history and quotes, using your token.

Those requests contain the stock symbols being looked up and your token, and are governed by Tradier’s own privacy policy. The app only reads market data; it does not place trades or access account balances or positions.

What Zoner Does Not Do

  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No crash reporting services
  • No advertising or tracking SDKs
  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, calendar, health, or motion data

Children

Zoner is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at github.com/ayefon/Zoner/issues.
