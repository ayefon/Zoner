Support

Getting started, common questions, and how to get in touch.

Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad running iOS 26 or later, or a Mac running macOS 26 or later
  • A Tradier API token for market data (a free sandbox token gives delayed data; a production token comes with a Tradier brokerage account)

Getting Started

1. Add your Tradier token

Open Settings → Tradier, choose the Account type that matches your token (Sandbox or Production), and paste the token. It’s stored only in your device’s Keychain.

2. Enter a symbol

Type a symbol in the field at the top of the list and press Return. Its chart appears with its supply and demand zones, and the symbol joins your list of the last 50 you entered. To add several at once, separate them with commas or spaces.

3. Choose a timeframe

The switch above the chart picks the candles: M W D 1h 15m 5m 1m (on iPhone, a menu). The expand button in the chart’s corner shows it full screen.

4. Look further back

Each chart searches back a year for zones (Settings → Charts → Zone Search, up to seven years) and opens on its recent candles. Drag the strip under the chart to scroll back, or drag its edges to show more or fewer candles; drag up or down on the prices at the left to see zones above or below. Under the chart, Nearest supply and Nearest demand name the closest zone each way, on screen or not.

5. Not ready for a token yet?

Tap Try with Sample Data in the list (or in Settings → Tradier) to explore every timeframe with made-up prices.

FAQ

It says Tradier rejected the token, or to add a token

Check that the Account setting matches the token: sandbox tokens only work with Sandbox, and production tokens only with Production. Use the eye button in Settings to check the saved token for typos.

“Tradier has no price history” for a symbol

Check the spelling. Zoner charts what Tradier carries: US stocks and funds. Share classes need their class letter, for example BRK.B (BRK/B works too).

What are the shaded and outlined bands?

Shaded bands are the chart’s own supply and demand zones: places where price paused for one to six candles before leaving sharply. Demand zones (price rallied away) use the up-candle color; supply zones (price dropped away) the down-candle color. Dashed outlines are the next timeframe up’s zones, labeled with its letter: M, W, D, H, 15m or 5m. Yellow circles mark where price came back to a zone, and fainter zones are weak: price didn’t leave them cleanly.

An intraday chart has few candles or none

The hourly and minute charts cover the regular session, 9:30 to 4:00 New York time, over the last 20 to 40 days that Tradier keeps. Over a weekend or holiday they end at the last session. Tap Refresh (⌘R on a keyboard) for the latest prices.

The times don’t match my clock

Intraday times are shown in New York time unless Settings → Charts → Intraday Times is set to Local Time.

The zones differ from another charting app

Zoner draws zones by fixed rules on Tradier’s prices. Some tools draw a zone’s near edge at the candles’ wicks; turn on Wicks above the chart to do the same.

A zone I expected isn’t on the chart

Zoner shows only zones price hasn’t closed through, found back as far as Zone Search reaches (a year unless you change it). Drag the strip under the chart to scroll back, drag the prices to look above or below, or check the Nearest lines under the chart.

Exported zones don’t show in thinkorswim or TradingView

Check that the chart there is the same symbol and one of Zoner’s seven timeframes (Monthly, Weekly, Daily, 1 hour, 15, 5 or 1 minute). The script is a snapshot: if a zone is missing that Zoner shows today, export it again.

There’s no IV gauge under the chart

The reason is shown there. The gauge needs options listed around 30 days out, and at least 282 days of prices (about 14 months), so recently listed stocks have none yet.

How do I remove a symbol from the list?

Swipe it (on the Mac, right-click it and choose Remove). To empty the whole list, use Clear All at the top of it. Entering a symbol again moves it back to the top of the list.

What’s kept if I delete the app?

On an iPhone or iPad, deleting the app erases your list of symbols and your settings on that device. Your Tradier token is kept in the Keychain and usually comes back after reinstalling; tap Remove in Settings → Tradier first if you don’t want that. On the Mac, deleting the app keeps everything.

Does the app place trades?

No. Zoner only reads market data. It never places orders, reads account balances or positions, or moves money.
