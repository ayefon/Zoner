Zoner

Supply and demand zone charts for iPhone, iPad, and Mac

What It Does

Type a stock or fund symbol and Zoner draws its candlestick chart with its supply and demand zones: the price areas where it paused before moving away sharply, which it often reacts to when it comes back.

Each chart shows its own zones and, outlined, the zones of the next timeframe up: weekly zones on the daily chart, daily zones on the hourly chart, and so on, from Monthly down to 1 Minute. Each chart searches back a year for zones (you can choose up to seven), opens on its recent candles, and scrolls back through the rest, and the nearest zone above and below the price is named under it. Zoner makes no predictions or recommendations; what to make of a zone stays with you.

The Zone Scan goes through a list of stocks, such as the S&P 500, and finds the ones falling toward a fresh demand zone or rising toward a fresh supply zone, within half a day’s average range of it, so you know which charts to study more closely.

Features

Seven timeframes: Monthly, Weekly, Daily, Hourly, 15, 5 and 1 Minute • Each chart’s zones plus the next timeframe’s, outlined • Zones searched back a year or more, with a strip under the chart to scroll back and the price axis to drag • The nearest supply and demand zones, how far away and when they formed • Zone patterns (RBR, DBR, RBD, DBD), tests marked where price came back, and weak zones drawn fainter or hidden • Zone edges at the base candles’ bodies or wicks • An IV gauge: today’s 30-day implied volatility against the past year’s historical volatility • Export Zones: every timeframe’s zones as a thinkorswim study or a TradingView indicator • Moving averages and a last-price line • A full-screen chart • Intraday times in New York time or your own time zone • Several symbols entered at once, and the last 50 kept in a list • The Zone Scan: stocks approaching a fresh zone on their daily or weekly chart, nearest first • Universes to scan: the S&P 500, Nasdaq-100 and 14 more, all editable, plus your own lists • Green and yellow bookmarks, with Bookmarked Only • Copy Tickers, Share Symbols and Share Results • A volume pane under the chart • Sample data to try the app without an account

Requirements

iPhone or iPad running iOS 26 or later, or a Mac running macOS 26 or later. Market data needs your own Tradier API token (a free sandbox token works, with delayed data).

Read-Only by Design

The app only reads market data: price history, quotes, and option volatility for the IV gauge. It never places orders, reads account balances or positions, or moves money. Zoner is an analysis tool, not investment advice.
